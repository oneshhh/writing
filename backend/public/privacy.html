Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

Real Write is an internal editorial workflow application. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used when you sign in and use the app.

Information We Collect

  • Account details: name and email address.
  • Authentication data: sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not store your plaintext password in the Real Write application database.
  • Usage and security logs: basic operational logs (for example: timestamps, request metadata) to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Cookies: we use secure, HTTP-only session cookies to keep you signed in and to protect the app from unauthorized access.
  • Payments (future): we may support payment data in the future. If that changes, we will update this policy before collecting any new categories of personal data.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and provide role-based access (admin/manager/writer).
  • To operate core product features such as projects, articles, reviews, and exports.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate security incidents and misuse.
  • To improve reliability (debugging, performance monitoring).

Sharing

We use trusted service providers to operate Real Write (for example, authentication and database services). We do not sell personal data. We only share information as needed to provide the service, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention

We retain account and operational data for as long as your account is active or as needed to operate the service. Access may be removed when no longer required for internal use.

Security

We use transport encryption (HTTPS in production), HTTP-only session cookies, and role-based authorization. Do not share your credentials, and sign out when using shared devices.

Contact

For access, corrections, or questions about this policy, contact the Real Write administrator for your organization.
